Why I Joined AltSchool

I chose to go to AltSchool due to a desire to get my hands dirty in software engineering and to build on my abilities outside of the classroom.

It's a program that will allow me to learn the frontend, backend and develop projects that are comparable to real software development.

I also joined with the idea that I would be able to be a part of a group of people interested in technology and software engineering. Collaborating with peers helps me to learn with them, share ideas, problem solve and grow as a developer.

The program will allow me to apply the knowledge that I have gained in computer science to the software development and apply it to my future internships, projects and career.
